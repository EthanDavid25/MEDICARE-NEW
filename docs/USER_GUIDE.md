# MEDICARE — User Guide
## Medical Symptoms Triage Advisor (Offline-First Desktop & Mobile)

---

## 1. Introduction
**MEDICARE** is a cross-platform (Android + Windows) preliminary healthcare assessment and medical navigation tool. It operates completely **offline**, allowing patients, caregivers, and first-responders to evaluate symptoms without an active internet connection.

> **Important Clinical Notice:** MEDICARE does not diagnose diseases, prescribe medications or dosages, or guarantee medical safety. Always seek in-person professional medical care for acute or concerning symptoms.

---

## 2. Navigating the Application

### 🏠 Dashboard
- Overview of application operating mode (100% Offline Autonomous).
- Summary of your active profile and recent assessments.
- Quick shortcut buttons to begin a new triage evaluation.

### 🩺 New Symptom Assessment
1. **Search & Select Symptoms:** Type to search the built-in library of 85+ validated symptoms (e.g., headache, chest discomfort, fever, dizziness).
2. **Rate Severity:** Use the 1–10 slider (1 = Mild/Manageable to 10 = Unbearable/Emergency).
3. **Record Duration:** Enter the number of hours or days the symptom has persisted.
4. **Trajectory:** Select whether the condition is **Worsening**, **Unchanged**, or **Improving**.
5. **Answer Clinical Questions:** The application generates focused, conditional follow-up questions linked to your specific symptoms, screening for emergency red-flag signs.

---

## 3. Understanding Your Triage Results

MEDICARE assigns one of four explainable urgency classifications:

| Category | Urgency Level | Meaning | Immediate Action Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Category 1** | 🚨 **Emergency Care** | Critical red-flag indicators or life-threatening symptoms identified. | Call emergency services (911 / 112 / 999) or attend nearest ER immediately. |
| **Category 2** | ⚠️ **Early Medical Consultation** | Progressive deterioration, high fever, or significant risk factors. | Seek evaluation at an Urgent Care center or GP clinic within 24–48 hours. |
| **Category 3** | 🟢 **Routine Healthcare** | Mild, stable, or resolving symptoms with no warning flags. | Standard primary care appointment or supportive self-care at home. |
| **Category 4** | ❓ **Insufficient Information** | Contradictory answers or missing required responses. | Re-evaluate with complete information or contact a health helpline. |

---

## 4. Doctor Handover Report
On the triage results screen or via the **Doctor Handover** menu:
- Click **"Generate Doctor Handover PDF"**.
- A clinical PDF summary is saved directly to your local device.
- Present this document to your attending doctor or triage nurse. It contains your vital demographics, known allergies, reported chief complaints, and triggered triage indicators.

---

## 5. Other Key Features
- **👤 Patient Profile:** Manage personal demographics, known drug allergies, chronic conditions, and emergency contacts.
- **📈 Progression Tracking:** Track changes in symptom severity across multiple evaluations.
- **🏥 Healthcare Directory:** Browse locally stored hospitals, emergency departments, and urgent care clinics with contact numbers and operating hours.
- **📚 Medical References:** Review educational medical guidance based on WHO ICD-11 classifications and emergency medicine standards.
- **⚙️ Settings:** Check local database paths, test network synchronization, and trigger manual backups.
